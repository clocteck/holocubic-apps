Default background music
========================

Title: Home in the Wilderness
Author: Herowl
Source project: VoxeLibre
Source file:
https://github.com/VoxeLibre/VoxeLibre/blob/master/mods/PLAYER/mcl_music/sounds/Herowl-Home_in_the_Wilderness.ogg

License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Modifications for this package:
- Downmixed from stereo to mono
- Resampled from 44100 Hz OGG to 11025 Hz PCM WAV
- Converted to signed 16-bit little-endian samples
- Added short fade-in/fade-out for click-free looping
- Peak level limited to -4 dBFS

The ClassiCube port and the BGM are separate works in a collection. This
converted BGM remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Official Minecraft/C418
music is not bundled. Users may replace bgm.wav with music they are authorised
to use, encoded as mono 16-bit PCM WAV at 11025 or 22050 Hz.
